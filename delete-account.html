Delete your Ourly account

Ourly by Nevermiss Studio

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open Ourly and tap the settings button at the top right.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete everything.
  3. Confirm.

Your account, the pair you're in and everything shared through it are deleted from our server right away, and the app on your phone is wiped.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous account ID and push token
  • Your pair, both public keys, and every encrypted item the two of you shared
  • Any open invite code
  • Everything stored in the app on your phone

Nothing is kept afterwards. Purchases made through Google Play stay in your Google account's purchase history, which Google keeps; to stop a subscription, cancel it in Google Play.

If you no longer have the app

Write to the contact email on Ourly's Google Play page. Because accounts are anonymous, uninstalling also makes your data unreachable: nobody can open the encrypted items without your phone's key. We delete your account and data when you ask.
