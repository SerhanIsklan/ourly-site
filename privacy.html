Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · Nevermiss Studio

The short version

Ourly is a private space for two people. Everything you share in it (moods, greetings, notes, drawings, plans, special days and cycle updates) is encrypted on your phone before it leaves, and only your phone and the phone of the person you paired with can open it. We can't read it, and nobody else can either.

For people without Ourly Plus, Ourly shows one banner ad on its home screen, served by Google AdMob. There are no ads on cycle screens and none at all with Plus. Ourly itself has no analytics and does no tracking.

What stays only on your phone

  • Your name, and the name you give the person you're paired with.
  • Your own cycle log, if you track your cycle. It is sent to your person only if you turn sharing on, and then only in encrypted form.
  • Your settings and the history of what you two shared.

What our server holds, and why

To deliver things between two phones, Ourly uses a server (Supabase, hosted in Tokyo, Japan). It holds only:

DataWhy
An anonymous account ID (no email, no phone number, no name)To know which phone is which
Who is paired with whom, and each phone's public encryption keyTo deliver to the right person and let your phones agree on a shared key
Encrypted items, with the time they were sent and their sizeTo deliver them, including when the other phone is offline. We can't read their content.
A short invite code, for 24 hoursSo the other person can pair with you
Your phone's push tokenTo wake the other phone when something arrives

Notifications are sent through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. The push itself carries no content, only "something new arrived". Your phone then fetches the encrypted item, opens it and writes the notification itself.

Purchases

Ourly Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your payment details. Whether Plus is on is shared with your person (encrypted), so one subscription covers both of you.

Health information

Cycle tracking is optional and off unless you turn it on. Your cycle log (period days, flow, symptoms and notes) stays on your phone. If you choose to share it, your person receives your current phase, period dates and your own tips, encrypted; never your flow, symptoms or notes. We never see any of it and never use it for anything, and it is never available to advertisers.

If you save a cycle backup, it's a file locked with a password you choose. You decide where it goes; we never receive it and can't open it.

Ads

The home-screen banner comes from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to personalise them where you agreed, Google may use your phone's advertising ID, IP address and general device information. Ads never see your moods, messages, drawings, plans or cycle: those are encrypted and never sent to Google. Where the law requires it, Ourly asks for your consent first, and Settings › Ad privacy choices lets you change it. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Keeping and deleting

  • Unpair: removes the pair and everything shared through it from our server.
  • Delete everything (Settings): deletes your account and everything tied to it from our server right away, then wipes the app on your phone. See how to delete your account.
  • Only your two phones hold the key. If a phone is lost or reset, its old messages can't be recovered by anyone, including us.

Sharing

We don't sell or share your data with anyone. The only services involved are Supabase (storage and delivery of encrypted items), Google Firebase (push wake-ups), Google Play (purchases) and Google AdMob (the home-screen banner, as described above).

Children

Ourly is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Your rights

You can delete your data at any time from the app. Under GDPR and Turkey's KVKK you can also ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, using the contact email on Ourly's Google Play page. Because we only hold anonymous IDs and encrypted data, we may ask you to send the request from inside the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
